Fare rules

  • Fares are non-transferable and valid only for the passenger named at booking.
  • Fares are quoted per person and include all applicable taxes and carrier-imposed surcharges.
  • Fare availability is subject to seat inventory and may change without notice.
  • Changes made within 24 hours of purchase are free of charge.
  • Changes made after 24 hours may incur a fee depending on fare class.
  • Same-day changes are subject to availability and any applicable fare difference.
  • Economy fares do not include a checked bag; Premium and Business include one.
  • Additional checked bags can be purchased at check-in or through Manage Booking.
  • Carry-on items must fit within the published cabin size limits.
  • No-shows forfeit the full value of the ticket.
  • Refund eligibility depends on the fare class purchased at the time of booking.
  • Refunds are issued to the original form of payment and may take several billing cycles to appear.
